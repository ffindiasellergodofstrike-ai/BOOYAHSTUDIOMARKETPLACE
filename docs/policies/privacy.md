## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide your account, digital orders and customer support:
• Account and contact details: Your name, email address, mobile/contact number and any optional profile details you provide. Authentication credentials are handled by the configured authentication service, including Supabase; passwords are not kept as readable text.
• Order and payment records: Purchased items, order IDs, payment references, amounts, payment status and timestamps.
• Download and security records: Available file-access events, download-link creation, IP addresses, user-agent information and timestamps used for security and order support.
• Communications: Contact-form messages, emails, WhatsApp messages and support records you send to us.
• Product updates: Your email and subscription record if you separately choose to subscribe to updates.
• Creator onboarding: public storefront name, bio and listings; private legal, identity, tax, payout and provider-onboarding details where needed. Sensitive payout details are encrypted with server-held keys and are not included in public profiles.
• Subscriptions: plan, renewal mode, mandate/provider references, verified paid periods, cancellation and billing history. We do not store bank mandate secrets or UPI PINs.

## 3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We use essential session cookies and local browser storage to keep you logged in, save items in your shopping cart/wishlist, and maintain secure navigation. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies.

## 4. Payment Processing & Service Providers

Payment information is entered on the payment provider’s checkout, including Cashfree, Razorpay and Easebuzz where enabled. Booyahstudio does not ask for or store card security codes (CVV), UPI PINs, banking passwords or payment OTPs. Necessary transaction references and payment status are retained to verify orders and provide support.

We share only the data necessary to operate the store with service providers for authentication and data storage (including Supabase), hosting (including Vercel), transactional email (including Resend), and payment processing (including Cashfree, Razorpay, Easebuzz and the original processor for an existing order). These services may process data outside your region according to their service terms and privacy practices. They are not appointed as alternative owners or grievance contacts for Booyahstudio.

For approved marketplace payments, necessary seller identity/payout details and the order’s split amounts are provided to the selected provider for onboarding, verification, settlement and compliance. A creator sees only the order information needed to fulfill and support their own sales; they cannot see another creator’s orders or private KYC. Relevant transaction and evidence records may be supplied to the original provider for a dispute. We never send customer passwords, service keys, UPI PINs or all customers’ data as payment metadata.
